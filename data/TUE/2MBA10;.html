2025-2026Sets, Logic and Mathematical Language (2MBA10)
Content
expand_moreexpand_less
Learning objectives and content
A first introduction to the language of mathematics and algebraic concepts.
The student knows the basic concepts and techniques of set theory, logic and abstract algebra can use it in calculations is familiar with logical reasoning.
The student can construct proofs on the topics mentioned.
The student can communicate within a mathematical setting
The student can experiment and conduct mathematical statements based on these experiments
 
Sets, constructions with sets, Logical operators, Quantifiers, functions and maps, (equivalence)relations, orders, linear recurrences. Induction and recursion. Computations with integers, in particular:
gcd, lcm, Euclidian algorithm, primes, Eratosthenes sieve, factorisation,
Chinese remainder theorem, modular arithmetic. Cardinalities.
Some practical applications, in particular wrt. digital communication.
